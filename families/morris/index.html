Morris

7 variants of the mill game. Nine Men's Morris, Lasker Morris, Morabaraba, Shax, and three size variants. Place your men, form a mill of three in a row, remove an opponent's piece. Then slide and trap.

Place, Mill, Remove

Morris is a two-phase game. First you place your men onto the intersections of concentric rings. Form a mill (three in a line) and remove one of your opponent's pieces. Once all men are placed, slide them along the lines to form new mills and trap your opponent.

Nine Men's Morris Rules

The classic form played since antiquity. Three concentric squares connected by midpoints. Nine men per side, placement phase then movement phase. Form a mill to remove an opponent's man.

engine:
  topology:
    type: graph
    graph: concentric-rings
    rings: 3
    midpoints: true
  players: [white, black]
  plugins:
    morris:
      menPerPlayer: 9
      flyingThreshold: 3

Nine Men's Morris — three rings, nine men, mill to capture

Lasker Morris Rules

Emanuel Lasker's improvement. Ten men per side and the option to place a new man or move an existing one on any turn, removing the strict placement-then-movement divide.

engine:
  topology:
    type: graph
    graph: concentric-rings
    rings: 3
    midpoints: true
  players: [white, black]
  plugins:
    morris:
      menPerPlayer: 10
      laskerRule: true
      flyingThreshold: 3

Lasker Morris — place or move on any turn
