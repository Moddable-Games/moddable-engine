Triangular

A board of triangles, declared as a picture: one string per rank, one character per cell, A for a triangle pointing up and V for one pointing down. Neighbouring triangles share their slanted sides.

1 Variants
44 Cells
2 Orientations

Drawn as It Is Played

The shape in the frontmatter is the source's own diagram, read cell by cell. The topology works out which triangles touch and which lines run straight; the shogi plugin moves its pieces along them.
