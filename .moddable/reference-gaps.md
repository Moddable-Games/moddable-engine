# Reference Gaps

Variants where parity compares two interpretations rather than implementation against rulebook, or where rules cannot be verified against a source.

| Variant | Status | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| `diceChess` | Playable, parity-excluded | No standard ruleset exists (Sunnucks documented, never codified). Our turn-loss rule is one defensible reading. Parity against MCE compares two arbitrary interpretations |
| `teleportChess` | **Blocked** | chessvariants.com returns 403. Three edge cases unresolved: (1) may the King teleport? (2) does a pawn teleporting to the last rank promote? (3) does teleporting reset the fifty-move counter. Do not reconstruct by inference; leave blocked until a source is accessible |
| `gustav-iii-chess` | Attribution unconfirmed | chessvariants.com 403. Billberg 1839 is specific but not independently verifiable. Gameplay unaffected |
